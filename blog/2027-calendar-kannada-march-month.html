Key Dates and Structure of March 2027

The Kannada calendar is lunisolar, so its months don’t always align perfectly with the Gregorian calendar. March 2027 typically covers parts of the months of Phalguna and Chaitra in the Kannada system.

Phalguna fades out as March begins, while Chaitra blooms toward the end, marking the start of the Kannada New Year around mid-April. But March is far from quiet—there’s a lot packed in.

March 2027 Kannada Month Breakdown

  • Phalguna Purnima (Full Moon Day) — This luminous day often falls in early March, celebrated for its spiritual significance.
  • Chaitra Masa Begins — The transition into Chaitra signals spring’s arrival and fresh beginnings.
  • Key Tithis (Lunar Days) — Panchami, Ashtami, and Ekadashi dates are crucial for religious rituals.

Festivals and Cultural Observances in March 2027

March’s calendar pulses with energy due to several iconic festivals rooted in Kannada culture. These occasions aren’t just dates; they’re expressions of history and faith.

Ugadi – The Kannada New Year Prelude

While Ugadi officially lands in April, the last days of March often see the community preparing for it with rituals and fasting. The anticipation builds as people clean houses and buy new clothes.

Holi – The Festival of Colors

Holi sometimes spills into March, depending on the lunar cycle. It’s a riotous celebration marking the triumph of good over evil and the joy of spring.

Other Notable Days

  • Ratha Saptami – Celebrated with fervor, it symbolizes the sun’s change of direction.
  • Amavasya (New Moon) – A day for ancestors’ remembrance and spiritual cleansing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the significance of Phalguna and Chaitra months in March?

Phalguna represents the closing phase of the year, often linked with harvest completion and festivities like Holi. Chaitra marks the new beginning, heralding spring and the Kannada New Year preparations.

How does the Kannada calendar differ from the Gregorian calendar in March?

Unlike the fixed Gregorian system, the Kannada calendar is lunisolar, adjusting months based on moon phases and solar transitions. This means March overlaps two different Kannada months, causing date shifts annually.

Can I use the Kannada calendar for business planning?

Absolutely. Many local businesses consult the Kannada calendar to pick auspicious dates for launching products or signing contracts, especially in 2026 and 2027 when traditions remain strong.
