How to Use This Calendar in Your Daily Life

Planning Outdoor Activities

Fishing enthusiasts swear by moon phases. The New Moon and Full Moon often bring peak fish activity. Mark these dates if you want to maximize your catch.

Boosting Creativity and Productivity

Writers and artists often find the waxing moon (from New to Full) fuels creativity, while the waning moon (Full to New) suits editing and reflection.

Gardening by the Moon

Planting during the waxing moon encourages growth, especially for leafy vegetables and flowers. The waning moon favors root crops and pruning.
