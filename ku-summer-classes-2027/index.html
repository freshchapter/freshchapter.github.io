Frequently Asked Questions About Ku Summer Classes 2027

Can I take summer classes if I’m not a KU student?

Yes, KU often allows non-degree or visiting students to enroll in summer classes, but you’ll need to check specific admission criteria and deadlines.

How many credits can I take during the summer?

Most students take between 6 to 9 credits, but this varies by program and your personal schedule. Overloading is possible but not recommended due to the fast pace.

Are summer classes easier than regular semester courses?

Not at all. They cover the same material but in a compressed timeframe, which means more work per week and less downtime.

What technology do I need for online courses?

A reliable computer, stable internet connection, and basic familiarity with KU’s learning management system are essential.
