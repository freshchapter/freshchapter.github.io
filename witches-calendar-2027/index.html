Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a sabbat and an esbat?

Sabbats are eight seasonal festivals marking Earth’s cycles, celebrated annually. Esbats are monthly gatherings or rituals aligned with the phases of the moon, mostly full moons.

Can I perform rituals on any moon phase?

While you can, certain moon phases amplify specific types of magic. New moons favor new beginnings; full moons boost manifestation and release.

How do eclipses affect magical work in 2027?

Eclipses intensify energy, often bringing sudden change. They’re powerful for transformation rituals but require extra grounding and protection.

Is the Witches Calendar 2027 different from a regular calendar?

Yes. It highlights mystical dates like sabbats, lunar phases, and planetary events essential for timing magic, which a regular calendar doesn’t show.

Do planetary retrogrades always bring bad energy?

Not necessarily. Retrogrades slow down communication and action, making them ideal for review and internal work rather than launching new projects.
