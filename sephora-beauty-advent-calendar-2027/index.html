Tips From a Beauty Insider: Maximizing Your Sephora Advent Calendar Experience

I’ve unboxed several of these calendars in my decade of covering beauty trends. Here’s what I’ve learned:

  1. Don’t hoard everything at once. Spread out the surprises to keep the excitement alive through December.
