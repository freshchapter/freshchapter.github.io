Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rely solely on the Low Fare Calendar for the cheapest flights?

The calendar is an excellent starting point but shouldn’t be your only tool. Combine it with fare alerts, flexible date searches, and sometimes even competitor price checks.

Do prices on the calendar include taxes and fees?

Generally, yes. United’s calendar shows total ticket price including taxes and fees, but always double-check before final booking.

How far in advance should I book using the calendar?

For domestic flights, 3 to 6 weeks ahead often yields the best deals. International flights may require booking 2 to 4 months out. The calendar helps you spot those sweet spots.

Does the calendar show prices for all fare classes?

No, it typically highlights the lowest available economy fares. If you want premium cabins, you’ll need to search separately.
