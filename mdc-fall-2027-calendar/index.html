How to Use the Calendar to Your Advantage

Here’s the thing: The calendar isn’t just dates on a page. It’s a tool to strategize your semester like a pro.

For example, snagging early registration slots lets you pick ideal class times before they fill up. I learned this the hard way—waiting even a day later meant settling for an 8 a.m. class I hated.

Step-by-Step Planning Guide

  1. Set reminders for priority registration—early birds get the best courses.
  2. Map out your weekly schedule immediately after registering to spot potential conflicts.
  3. Note deadlines for adding, dropping, or withdrawing to avoid penalties and stress.
  4. Block study sessions around exam weeks noted in the calendar.
  5. Keep the calendar handy on your phone or planner for quick reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does priority registration start for Fall 2027?

Priority registration for Fall 2027 at MDC begins on June 1, 2027, and runs through June 15, 2027. It’s the best time to secure your preferred classes.

Can I add or drop classes after the semester starts?

Yes, you can add or drop classes without penalty until August 31, 2027. After that, withdrawals are possible but may have academic or financial consequences.

What happens if I miss the withdrawal deadline?

Missing the October 15, 2027 withdrawal deadline means you’ll likely receive a grade that impacts your GPA. It’s crucial to plan ahead and communicate with advisors if you’re struggling.
