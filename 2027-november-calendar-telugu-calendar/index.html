Understanding the Telugu Calendar System This November

The Telugu calendar is lunisolar—it blends lunar phases with solar movements, creating a rhythm that syncs with nature’s cycles. November 2027 corresponds mainly to the months of Kartika and Agrahayana in the Telugu calendar.

Each day is marked by its unique tithi (lunar day), nakshatra (star constellation), and yoga (planetary combination), all crucial for determining auspicious moments.
