How to Choose the Best Sephora Advent Calendar for You

Don’t just grab the first calendar you see. Think about your beauty priorities and daily habits. Are you more skincare or makeup? Do you love trying new brands or stick to staples?

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Sephora Advent Calendars 2027 usually become available?

Typically, Sephora drops their Advent Calendars in early November, sometimes even late October. The best strategy is to watch for announcements in October and be ready to buy as soon as they launch.

Are the products in the calendars full-size or samples?

Calendars usually contain a mix. Some items are deluxe miniatures, while others might be full-size. It varies by calendar, so check product details before purchasing.

Can I buy Sephora Advent Calendars in-store or only online?

Both options are valid, but online shopping often offers the first chance to grab them. Stores tend to get limited stock, so if you want one, online is your safest bet.

Are Sephora Advent Calendars worth the price?

Absolutely. In my experience, the value of products inside often exceeds the calendar price by a significant margin, especially considering the exclusives and deluxe samples.
