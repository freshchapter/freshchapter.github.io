Printing Tips and Tricks for a Flawless July 2027 Calendar

Printing the calendar sounds straightforward until the page scaling or margins throw you off. Here’s what I’ve learned after countless print jobs: always set your printer to “actual size” or 100%, never “fit to page.”

Use quality paper—cardstock works wonders for durability. It’s like giving your calendar a VIP pass to your daily hustle.

Choosing the Right Printer Settings

  1. Set orientation to landscape for wider writing space.
  2. Disable any “shrink to fit” settings to preserve layout integrity.
  3. Opt for color printing if you want to highlight weekends or holidays.

Don’t have a printer? Local print shops or office supply stores can print for you. Just bring the file on a USB, and you’re set. Easy.
