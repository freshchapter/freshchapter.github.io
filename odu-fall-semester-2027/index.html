Don’t underestimate how a killer professor or a subject you actually enjoy can flip your semester from slog to stride. Mix hard classes with lighter ones to keep your energy up.

Electives aren’t just filler; they’re your chance to explore or build skills that boost your resume. For Odu Fall Semester 2027, check out courses in emerging tech or communication — they’re crowd-pleasers and practical.
