Preservation Tips: Keep Your Class Of 2027 Picture Frame Looking Fresh

Graduation is a one-time event, but your memories should last forever. Protecting your frame from fading and damage is crucial.

Avoid Direct Sunlight

Sunlight can bleach photos and warp frames, especially wood. Place your frame where it catches indirect light or use UV-protective glass.

Regular Dusting and Cleaning

A soft, lint-free cloth is your best friend. Avoid harsh chemicals that can strip finishes or damage prints.
