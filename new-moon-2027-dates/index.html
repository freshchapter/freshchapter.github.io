Quick Facts About New Moons You Probably Didn’t Know

📑 Quick Facts Box:

  • The lunar cycle is about 29.5 days long, making new moons nearly monthly events.
  • A new moon occurs when the moon is positioned between Earth and the Sun, making it invisible from Earth.
  • During a new moon, the night sky is at its darkest, ideal for deep-sky observations.
  • New moons are often linked with cultural and spiritual practices worldwide.
  • “Black moon” events, like September 2027’s second new moon, are quite rare and special.

Frequently Asked Questions About New Moon Dates in 2027

What time zone are the new moon times listed in?

The times provided are in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). You’ll want to convert them to your local time zone for precise observation.

Can I see the new moon with the naked eye?

No, the new moon itself is not visible because it’s between Earth and the Sun. But the nights around the new moon are perfect for seeing stars and other celestial events.

Why does September 2027 have two new moons?

Because the lunar cycle is shorter than most months, occasionally two new moons fall in a single calendar month. This second new moon is called a “black moon.”
